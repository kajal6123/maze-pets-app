Privacy Policy for Maze Pets

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Maze Pets is a mobile maze puzzle game developed by Ojal Acrade.

1. Information We Collect

Maze Pets does not require users to create an account or directly provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or address.

The game may store gameplay information locally on the user's device, including level progress, stars, coins, and game settings.

2. Advertising

Maze Pets displays advertisements using Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect or use information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, and information related to advertising and app interaction to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable laws, user settings, and consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

3. Third-Party Services

Maze Pets uses Google AdMob for advertising. Maze Pets itself does not sell users' personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

Maze Pets does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and data practices are configured according to applicable Google Play and advertising requirements.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. However, no electronic method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or Maze Pets, users may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Maze Pets Google Play Store listing.
